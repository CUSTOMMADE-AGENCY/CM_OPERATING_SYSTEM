# ClickUp Task Template — Monthly Management

## Core rule
GitHub defines the process. ClickUp executes the process. Drive stores deliverables. Gmail handles communication.

## Purpose
De maandelijkse Artist Management-cyclus uitvoeren: voortgang reviewen, decisions vastleggen, prioriteiten bepalen, risks escaleren en ClickUp execution bijwerken.

## When to use
Gebruik deze template iedere maand voor actieve Artist Management-clients en extra wanneer een maand veel releases, deals, partnerships of blockers bevat.

## ClickUp Space
`Artist Management Clients`

## ClickUp List
`AM - [Artist Name] - Monthly Management - [YYYY-MM]`

## Statuses
- `Preparation`
- `Inputs verzamelen`
- `Monthly review`
- `In uitvoering`
- `Wacht op artist approval`
- `Follow-up`
- `Escalated`
- `Approved`
- `Completed`
- `Blocked`

## Task title format
`AM | [Artist Name] | Monthly Management | [YYYY-MM]`

## Required custom fields
| Field | Type | Required value |
|---|---|---|
| GitHub Workflow | URL | `docs/02_ARTIST_MANAGEMENT/06_MONTHLY_MANAGEMENT_FLOW.md` |
| Drive Folder | URL | Monthly Management month folder |
| Gmail Template Set | Dropdown | Meeting Agenda; Meeting Recap; Monthly Summary; Approval Request |
| Approval Owner | Person | Artist manager + decision owner |
| Month | Date/text | `YYYY-MM` |
| Active Priorities | Multi-select/text | Current P0/P1 items |
| Decisions Needed | Number/text | Count/list of decision items |
| Escalation Required | Checkbox | Checked if blocker needs decision |
| Roadmap Updated | Checkbox | Checked before completion |
| Required Evidence | Text | Summary, decision log, risk log, updated roadmap |

## Subtasks
1. Vorige maand reviewen: completed work, missed deadlines en open blockers.
2. Active workstreams uit Artist Roadmap en ClickUp reviewen.
3. Artist goals, constraints, calendar en capacity voor komende maand bevestigen.
4. Rights, royalties, deals, releases, partnerships en finance items nalopen.
5. Prioriteiten en non-prioriteiten voor volgende maand bepalen.
6. Decisions, approvals, Scope changes en budget items vastleggen.
7. ClickUp owners, dates, dependencies en statuses bijwerken.
8. Risk/escalation log actualiseren.
9. Monthly summary schrijven en versturen.
10. Evidence en approvals archiveren in Drive.

## Checklist
- [ ] Meeting agenda is verstuurd.
- [ ] Vorige maand is gereviewd.
- [ ] Overdue items hebben owner en escalation path.
- [ ] Elke komende maand priority heeft owner en due date.
- [ ] Decision log is bijgewerkt.
- [ ] Risk/escalation log is bijgewerkt.
- [ ] Scope changes zijn gemarkeerd en approved vóór execution.
- [ ] Artist Roadmap is bijgewerkt.
- [ ] Monthly Summary is verstuurd.
- [ ] Drive folder bevat alle evidence.

## Approval owner
Artist manager voor monthly cycle en ClickUp integrity. Artist of aangewezen decision owner voor priorities, budgetten, public commitments en strategic decisions.
