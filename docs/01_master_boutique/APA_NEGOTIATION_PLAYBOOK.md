# APA Negotiation Playbook

## Purpose

Guide negotiation of asset purchase agreements for catalog, master and publishing transactions.

## Negotiation areas

- Purchased assets and excluded assets.
- Purchase price, payment timing and holdbacks.
- Representations and warranties.
- Indemnities, caps, baskets and survival periods.
- Consents, third-party approvals and notices.
- Transition assistance, royalties and post-closing collections.
- Closing deliverables and conditions precedent.

## Process

1. Confirm LOI terms and deal assumptions.
2. Align with counsel on drafting responsibility.
3. Map open business points before legal markup.
4. Track issues by owner, risk level and deadline.
5. Escalate economics, liability and title issues quickly.
6. Confirm closing checklist before signing.

## Storage and tooling boundaries
- GitHub stores this workflow, governance, SOP and system documentation.
- Google Drive stores live client folders, signed documents, deliverables and working files.
- ClickUp stores execution, pipeline stages, owners, due dates and task evidence.
- Gmail stores correspondence and approved communication templates.

## Repository hygiene
- Do not commit signed contracts, confidential client files, exports, duplicate folders or ad hoc drafts.
- Use clear version history through commits and pull requests instead of `final_final` filenames.
- Keep client-specific details in the appropriate live workspace, not in this repository.
