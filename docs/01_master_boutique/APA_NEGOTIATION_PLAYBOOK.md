# APA Negotiation Playbook

## Purpose

Guide negotiation of asset purchase agreements for catalog, master and publishing transactions.

## Scope

This playbook applies after an LOI, term sheet or approved deal framework is ready to move into asset purchase agreement negotiation. It covers business-point alignment, issue tracking, escalation and closing readiness. Legal counsel owns legal drafting and legal advice.

## Owner

The deal lead owns the business negotiation process, issue log and internal alignment. Counsel owns legal drafting, legal risk analysis and agreement language. The client or designated decision owner approves material economics, liability, title and closing positions.

## Inputs

- Executed LOI, term sheet or approved business framework.
- Diligence checklist, valuation memo and issue log.
- Asset schedules, ownership summaries and revenue support.
- Buyer or seller comments, counsel markup and open issue list.
- Required consents, notices, tax forms, payment instructions and closing deliverables.
- Internal approval thresholds for economics, liability and risk allocation.

## Negotiation areas

- Purchased assets and excluded assets.
- Purchase price, payment timing and holdbacks.
- Representations and warranties.
- Indemnities, caps, baskets and survival periods.
- Consents, third-party approvals and notices.
- Transition assistance, royalties and post-closing collections.
- Closing deliverables and conditions precedent.

## Process

1. Confirm LOI terms and deal assumptions.
2. Align with counsel on drafting responsibility.
3. Map open business points before legal markup.
4. Track issues by owner, risk level and deadline.
5. Escalate economics, liability and title issues quickly.
6. Confirm closing checklist before signing.

## Process steps

1. Translate LOI terms into a business-point checklist before reviewing long-form drafts.
2. Confirm the asset perimeter, excluded assets, schedules and revenue periods with diligence support.
3. Assign each open issue to business, legal, tax, accounting or rights ownership.
4. Review markups against approved economics, risk tolerance and closing timeline.
5. Escalate deviations from approved price, payment timing, holdback, indemnity or title positions.
6. Track consents, notices, payor changes, transition obligations and post-closing collections.
7. Resolve or intentionally allocate diligence exceptions in schedules, reps, covenants or indemnities.
8. Obtain decision-owner approval for material concessions before communicating acceptance.
9. Confirm signature packets, closing deliverables, funds flow and post-closing handoff before signing.

## Outputs

- APA issue log with owner, status, risk level and next action.
- Business-points summary for counsel and decision owners.
- Closing checklist with required deliverables and responsible parties.
- Approved negotiation positions for economics, liability, title and transition matters.
- Post-closing handoff notes for collections, notices and ongoing obligations.

## Quality checks

- Long-form terms match the approved LOI or documented deviations.
- Purchased and excluded assets are clear enough to avoid post-closing ambiguity.
- Material concessions are approved before they are accepted.
- Diligence exceptions are resolved, allocated or explicitly accepted.
- Closing deliverables, funds flow and signature authority are confirmed before signing.
- Legal advice and legal drafting remain with counsel.

Review this playbook at the start of each APA negotiation, after major deal lessons learned and semiannually with counsel or the deal team.
