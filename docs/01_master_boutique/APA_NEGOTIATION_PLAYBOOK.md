# APA Negotiation Playbook

## Purpose

Guide CM through asset purchase agreement review and negotiation support.

## Scope

Applies to Master Boutique transaction work only. Confidential deal files, signed documents, financial statements, and data-room materials stay in Drive or the approved portal.

## Inputs

- Accepted LOI or commercial terms
- Draft APA
- Disclosure schedule
- Rights and revenue diligence findings

## Workflow

1. Map APA terms against LOI.
2. Flag purchase price, asset scope, warranties, indemnity, conditions, and closing mechanics.
3. Coordinate legal review in Drive.
4. Track open points in ClickUp.
5. Do not close until commercial and legal conditions are reconciled.

## Outputs

- Updated ClickUp status and notes.
- Required Drive materials stored in the correct deal folder.
- Clear next action, owner, and deadline.

## Quality standard

The process is complete only when the next stage has enough documented evidence to proceed without rework.

## Version

V1.0 starter operating standard.
