# Catalog Valuation Playbook

## Purpose

Create a practical valuation view for catalog and rights opportunities.

## Scope

Applies to Master Boutique transaction work only. Confidential deal files, signed documents, financial statements, and data-room materials stay in Drive or the approved portal.

## Inputs

- Revenue statements
- Ownership splits
- Term and territory
- Growth or decay trend
- Comparable market context

## Workflow

1. Normalize revenue by period and source.
2. Separate master, publishing, neighbouring, and other income streams.
3. Identify exclusions and recoupment issues.
4. Apply conservative, base, and upside scenarios.
5. Document assumptions before buyer discussions.

## Outputs

- Updated ClickUp status and notes.
- Required Drive materials stored in the correct deal folder.
- Clear next action, owner, and deadline.

## Quality standard

The process is complete only when the next stage has enough documented evidence to proceed without rework.

## Version

V1.0 starter operating standard.
