# Buyer Qualification Model

## Purpose

Evaluate whether a buyer is suitable before sensitive deal access is granted.

## Scope

Applies to Master Boutique transaction work only. Confidential deal files, signed documents, financial statements, and data-room materials stay in Drive or the approved portal.

## Inputs

- Buyer identity and entity
- Acquisition mandate
- Proof of funds or track record
- Rights appetite
- Decision-maker access

## Workflow

1. Confirm buyer category and investment focus.
2. Check conflict, territory, and rights fit.
3. Confirm NDA completion before sensitive materials.
4. Record qualification notes in ClickUp.
5. Approve or reject data-room access.

## Outputs

- Updated ClickUp status and notes.
- Required Drive materials stored in the correct deal folder.
- Clear next action, owner, and deadline.

## Quality standard

The process is complete only when the next stage has enough documented evidence to proceed without rework.

## Version

V1.0 starter operating standard.
