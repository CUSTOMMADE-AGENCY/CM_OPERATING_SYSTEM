# Dealflow Catalog Asset

## Purpose

Qualify and structure inbound catalog, master, publishing, and rights opportunities before buyer outreach.

## Scope

Applies to Master Boutique transaction work only. Confidential deal files, signed documents, financial statements, and data-room materials stay in Drive or the approved portal.

## Inputs

- Introducer or seller name
- Asset description and territory
- Rights type and ownership claim
- Revenue history and available statements
- Seller expectation and timing

## Workflow

1. Open a ClickUp task at LEAD or INTRODUCER.
2. Request asset basics and ownership evidence.
3. Classify the opportunity by rights type and deal objective.
4. Check whether materials are complete enough for review.
5. Move to RIGHTS REVIEW only when minimum evidence is present.

## Outputs

- Updated ClickUp status and notes.
- Required Drive materials stored in the correct deal folder.
- Clear next action, owner, and deadline.

## Quality standard

The process is complete only when the next stage has enough documented evidence to proceed without rework.

## Version

V1.0 starter operating standard.
