# Google Drive Structure

Google Drive stores live operating files that should not live in GitHub, including signed documents, deliverables, templates, assets, finance files, legal files, and client folders.

## Root folders

- `00_ADMIN` — company administration, internal references, non-client operational records, and approved internal templates.
- `01_MASTER_BOUTIQUE` — transaction working materials, reusable deal templates, buyer-facing non-confidential templates, and internal transaction resources.
- `02_ARTIST_MANAGEMENT` — artist-service templates, brand audit resources, brandbook resources, roadmap templates, and management delivery assets.
- `03_CLIENTS` — live client folders for approved client work and deliverables.
- `04_DEALS` — live deal folders, deal-specific working files, buyer process folders, and closing support files.
- `05_OPERATIONS` — operating assets, meeting materials, internal process support, and administrative execution files.
- `06_FINANCE` — invoices, financial admin, payment evidence, and finance exports.
- `07_LEGAL` — legal templates, signed agreements, counsel-reviewed files, and legal execution copies.
- `08_MARKETING` — marketing assets, positioning files, public collateral, and campaign materials.
- `09_CONTENT` — content assets, creative files, media, and publishing-ready materials.
- `99_ARCHIVE` — inactive clients, closed deals, old versions, and retired operating materials.

## Client folder template

Each approved client folder inside `03_CLIENTS` uses this structure:

- `01_ADMIN` — contracts, onboarding records, billing setup, and administrative notes.
- `02_BRAND` — brand audit materials, brand references, positioning, visual direction, and brandbook drafts.
- `03_STRATEGY` — strategy documents, roadmaps, release plans, and campaign direction.
- `04_DEALS_RIGHTS` — rights reviews, deal notes, royalty materials, and opportunity tracking.
- `05_RELEASES` — release-specific plans, assets, timelines, and coordination files.
- `06_CONTENT` — content planning, creative assets, production files, and approvals.
- `07_FINANCE` — client-specific invoices, budgets, royalty statements, and payment records.
- `08_DELIVERABLES` — final client-facing deliverables and approved handover files.
- `09_ARCHIVE` — inactive drafts, old versions, and completed-phase material.

## Rules

- Use uppercase folder names.
- Avoid duplicate folders and parallel structures.
- Do not create deep unnecessary nesting.
- Keep signed, confidential, legal, finance, and deliverable files in Drive, not GitHub.
