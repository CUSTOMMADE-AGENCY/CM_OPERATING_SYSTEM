# Document Standards

## Minimum content

Every operating document should include:

- Purpose.
- Scope.
- Owner or accountable lane.
- Inputs and outputs.
- Workflow steps or checklist.
- Quality standard.
- Version note when released.

## Writing standard

- Write in clear operational language.
- Prefer checklists, tables, and short decision rules.
- Separate policy from procedure.
- Keep confidential examples out of GitHub.

## Review standard

Documents should be reviewed when a process changes, when a tool setup changes, or when a recurring delivery issue shows the standard is unclear.
