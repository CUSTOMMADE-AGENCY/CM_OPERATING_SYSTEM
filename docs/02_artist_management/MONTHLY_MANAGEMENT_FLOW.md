# Monthly Management Flow

## Purpose

Create a predictable monthly operating rhythm for artist management clients.

## Scope

This flow applies to monthly retainers and active management projects that require recurring planning, reporting and accountability. It covers preparation, review, decision capture, follow-up and monthly close-out.

## Owner

The artist manager owns the monthly cycle and summary. The artist owns priority decisions and approvals. Workstream owners provide status updates and evidence for their assigned deliverables.

## Inputs

- Current artist roadmap and previous monthly summary.
- ClickUp task list, completed work and overdue items.
- Release, content, touring, sync, brand, publishing or catalog timelines.
- Open approvals, risks, invoices, budget items and commercial pipeline notes.
- Advisor updates from legal, accounting, business affairs, distribution or marketing.
- Artist feedback, constraints and upcoming availability.

## Monthly cycle

- Review current goals and active projects.
- Update roadmap and deadlines.
- Review open decisions and approvals.
- Check rights, royalties and deal items.
- Review budget, invoices or commercial pipeline where relevant.
- Confirm next month priorities.

## Process steps

1. Prepare a pre-read with completed work, open items, decisions needed and risk flags.
2. Review the previous month's commitments against completed deliverables and blockers.
3. Confirm the artist's current goals, creative priorities and availability constraints.
4. Update active workstreams, deadlines, owners and dependencies.
5. Review rights, royalties, contracts, budget or commercial issues that require escalation.
6. Decide the next month's priority outcomes and deprioritized items.
7. Convert decisions into ClickUp tasks with due dates, owners and required evidence.
8. Send a monthly summary with decisions, next actions, risks and follow-up owners.
9. File relevant deliverables, summaries and final documents in the appropriate system of record.

## Outputs

- Monthly management summary.
- Updated roadmap and priority list.
- Updated ClickUp tasks with owners, due dates and evidence requirements.
- Decision log entries for approvals, deferrals and escalations.
- Risk or scope-change notes where follow-up is required.

## Quality checks

- The monthly summary clearly separates completed work, open decisions and next actions.
- No active priority lacks an owner or due date.
- Deferred work is intentionally parked, not forgotten.
- Rights, royalty, legal, accounting and budget issues are routed to qualified owners.
- The next month's priorities fit within the approved scope and available capacity.
- The artist receives one clear source of truth after the review.

## System of record

- GitHub stores this workflow, governance, SOP and system documentation.
- Google Drive stores live client folders, signed documents, deliverables and working files.
- ClickUp stores execution, pipeline stages, owners, due dates and task evidence.
- Gmail stores correspondence and approved communication templates.

## Review cadence

Run this flow every calendar month for active management clients. Review the flow quarterly to ensure it still matches the management offer, reporting expectations and tool setup.

## Repository hygiene

- Do not commit signed contracts, confidential client files, exports, duplicate folders or ad hoc drafts.
- Use clear version history through commits and pull requests instead of `final_final` filenames.
- Keep client-specific details in the appropriate live workspace, not in this repository.
