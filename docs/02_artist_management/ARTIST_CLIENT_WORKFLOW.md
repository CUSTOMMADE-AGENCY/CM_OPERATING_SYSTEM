# Artist Client Workflow

## Purpose

Manage an artist relationship from lead to close-out or next phase.

## Scope

Applies to Artist Management client-service work only. Client-confidential materials, signed contracts, and final deliverables stay in the approved Google Drive client folder.

## Inputs

- Lead source
- Artist profile
- Service interest
- Budget and timing

## Workflow

1. Log lead in ClickUp.
2. Run fit check before proposing.
3. Sequence brand audit, brandbook, business audit, scope, and proposal.
4. Confirm contract and payment before onboarding.
5. Deliver, review, close out, and propose the next phase where relevant.

## Outputs

- Updated ClickUp status and task ownership.
- Correct files stored in the client Drive folder.
- Written client decision, approval, or next step.

## Quality standard

No strategy or execution work starts until the required foundation and approvals are documented.

## Version

V1.0 starter operating standard.
