# Buyer Qualification Model

## Purpose

Qualify prospective buyers before sharing sensitive catalog or asset information.

## Qualification dimensions

| Dimension | Questions |
|---|---|
| Strategic fit | Does the buyer actively acquire this asset class, territory and revenue profile? |
| Financial capacity | Can the buyer credibly fund the likely purchase price and closing costs? |
| Execution history | Has the buyer closed similar transactions on schedule? |
| Confidentiality | Is the buyer willing to sign the approved NDA and respect process rules? |
| Diligence readiness | Can the buyer review statements, metadata and legal materials professionally? |
| Relationship quality | Is the buyer responsive, direct and aligned with seller expectations? |

## Scoring

Score each dimension from 1 to 5. Buyers below 3 in confidentiality or financial capacity should not receive data-room access.

## Storage and tooling boundaries

- Google Drive stores live client folders, signed documents, deliverables and working files.

## Repository hygiene
- Do not commit signed contracts, confidential client files, exports, duplicate folders or ad hoc drafts.
- Use clear version history through commits and pull requests instead of `final_final` filenames.
- Keep client-specific details in the appropriate live workspace, not in this repository.
