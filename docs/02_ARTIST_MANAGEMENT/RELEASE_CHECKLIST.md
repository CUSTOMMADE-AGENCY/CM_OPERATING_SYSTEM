# Release Checklist

## Doel

Gebruik deze checklist om release-readiness te controleren vóór campagne-execution, go-live en post-release review.

## Pre-campaign checks

| Check | Owner | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Release objective, target audience en positioning zijn bevestigd. | Artist manager | Release strategy document. |
| Release date, distributor deadlines en asset deadlines zijn vastgelegd. | Release owner | ClickUp milestones. |
| Master, artwork, metadata en credits zijn final of hebben duidelijke owner/deadline. | Release owner | Drive files and tasks. |
| Splits, samples, publishing en neighbouring-rights issues zijn gecontroleerd. | Rights owner | Rights notes or counsel/accounting input. |
| Budget, spend approvals en paid media boundaries zijn bevestigd. | Budget owner | Written approval. |
| Content plan, press angles en platform priorities zijn bepaald. | Artist manager | Campaign plan. |
| Announcement copy, captions, visuals en public language zijn approved. | Artist | Approval evidence. |
| External partners, distributor, PR, playlisting, content creators of brand partners hebben tasks. | Artist manager | ClickUp tasks. |

## Launch checks

| Check | Owner | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| DSP links, smartlink en platform profiles zijn gecontroleerd. | Release owner | Links in ClickUp. |
| Launch assets staan in Google Drive met approved versions. | Artist manager | Drive folder. |
| Day-of-release communication is scheduled or assigned. | Artist manager | Content calendar. |
| Blockers zijn resolved, escalated of accepted in writing. | Artist manager | Risk log. |

## Post-release checks

| Check | Owner | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Performance review is gepland. | Artist manager | Calendar/ClickUp task. |
| Learnings, wins, blockers en next actions zijn vastgelegd. | Artist manager | Monthly review or post-release report. |
| Follow-up content, pitching, partnerships of deal opportunities zijn geprioriteerd. | Artist manager | Roadmap update. |
