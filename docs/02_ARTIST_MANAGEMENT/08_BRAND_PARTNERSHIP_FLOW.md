# 08 BRAND PARTNERSHIP FLOW

## Doel

Deze Workflow definieert hoe CM brand partnership opportunities voor artists identificeert, beoordeelt, structureert, onderhandelt en overdraagt naar execution. Het doel is om creative fit, brand safety, commercial terms, approvals, deliverables, usage rights en reputation risk te bewaken.

De Workflow borgt dat partnerships de artist positioning versterken en niet alleen korte-termijn inkomsten opleveren.

## Reikwijdte

Deze Workflow geldt voor sponsored content, ambassador deals, campaign collaborations, product seeding met verplichtingen, live activations, sync-adjacent brand uses, social campaigns, event partnerships en long-term brand relationships.

Deze Workflow omvat opportunity management, Fit Check, briefing, negotiation coordination, approval en handover. Legal drafting, tax advice en production execution vallen alleen binnen Scope wanneer expliciet approved.

## Eigenaar

De brand partnership lead of artist manager owns de Workflow, Pipeline status, Fit Check, briefing, approvals, negotiation tracker en handover. De artist owns creative approval, brand fit approval en final commercial approval. Counsel owns legal advice en contract language waar relevant.

## Inputs

- Artist Brandbook, Brand Audit en artist roadmap.
- Partnership Lead, brand brief, agency contact en campaign objective.
- Proposed deliverables, channels, timing, usage, exclusivity, fee en reporting requirements.
- Artist boundaries, category restrictions, values en content comfort.
- Existing sponsorships, conflicts, label/distributor restrictions en management obligations.
- Audience data, press kit, Assets en prior partnership examples.
- Legal, finance en tax questions waar relevant.

## Workflow stappen

### 1. Registreer Lead in Pipeline

Leg brand, agency, source, campaign type, deadline, proposed fee, status, owner, next action en confidentiality level vast.

### 2. Voer partnership Fit Check uit

Beoordeel artist fit, values fit, audience fit, category fit, creative fit, timing, budget, reputation risk en opportunity cost.

### 3. Check restrictions en conflicts

Controleer exclusivity, existing obligations, label/distributor restrictions, rights limitations, competing categories en public sensitivity.

### 4. Definieer partnership concept

Vertaal brand objective naar artist-authentic concept, deliverables, platforms, tone, usage boundaries, content approvals en success measures.

### 5. Bepaal commercial terms

Bevestig fee, payment schedule, usage rights, term, territory, exclusivity, whitelisting, paid media, revisions, cancellation, reporting en expenses.

### 6. Coördineer negotiation en legal review

Brief counterparty en counsel met terms, risks, open questions en approval requirements. Leg alle material terms schriftelijk vast.

### 7. Verkrijg artist approval

Laat artist concept, brand, deliverables, usage, fee, timing, exclusivity en public-facing language approves vóór commitment.

### 8. Draag over naar execution

Maak ClickUp tasks voor deliverables, Assets, deadlines, approvals, posting, reporting, invoicing en proof of performance.

### 9. Evalueer partnership

Review performance, brand relationship, audience response, payment status, learnings en future Upsell of renewal opportunity.

## Beslismomenten

- Past de brand bij artist positioning en Brandbook?
- Zijn deliverables haalbaar binnen timing en capacity?
- Is exclusivity acceptabel voor de artist roadmap?
- Zijn usage rights en paid media duidelijk begrensd?
- Is counsel review verplicht vóór signature?
- Wordt de Lead voortgezet, geparkeerd of afgewezen?

## Deliverables

- Partnership Fit Check.
- Partnership brief.
- Term sheet of deal memo.
- Approval record.
- Execution checklist.
- Reporting/proof of performance package.
- Post-partnership evaluation.

## KPI's

- 100% van partnership Leads heeft Pipeline status en next action.
- Geen brand commitment zonder artist approval.
- Usage rights, exclusivity en deliverables zijn schriftelijk bevestigd.
- Deliverables worden volgens approved timeline opgeleverd.
- Payment en reporting follow-up zijn zichtbaar in ClickUp.

## Escalaties

Escaleren bij reputation risk, category conflict, unclear usage rights, broad exclusivity, low fee/high obligation, unapproved creative change, missed deliverable, payment delay, public backlash, counsel concern of Scope conflict.

## Outputs

- Approved partnership deal of declined opportunity record.
- Updated artist roadmap en Pipeline.
- ClickUp execution tasks.
- Google Drive archive met Assets, approvals en final documents.
- Learnings voor toekomstige partnership outreach.
