# 05 RELEASE STRATEGIE FLOW

## Doel

Deze Workflow definieert hoe CM een Release Strategie ontwikkelt en bestuurt voor een artist. Het doel is om creative direction, rights readiness, positioning, audience, content, budget, distribution, approvals en launch operations vóór public commitment op elkaar af te stemmen.

Een goede Release Strategie voorkomt last-minute execution, onduidelijke messaging, rights blockers, missing Assets, budgetoverschrijding en inconsistent campaign management.

## Reikwijdte

Deze Workflow geldt voor singles, EP's, albums, deluxe releases, re-releases, featured releases en campaign moments waar CM een management- of strategyrol heeft.

De Workflow omvat strategy, planning, coordination en launch readiness. Distribution delivery, legal drafting, accounting en paid media buying vallen alleen binnen Scope wanneer expliciet approved.

## Eigenaar

De artist manager owns de Release Strategie-Workflow, timeline, owners, approvals, task hygiene en escalation. De artist owns creative approval, release commitment, public-facing language en budget approval. Label, distributor, PR, marketing, creative of counsel own specialist deliverables wanneer toegewezen.

## Inputs

- Artist roadmap, goals en audience priorities.
- Tracklist, masters, artwork, video, photos, lyrics en campaign Assets.
- Master Rights, Publishing Rights, split sheets, metadata, ISRC/UPC en distribution status.
- Brandbook, Brand Audit-output en existing press materials.
- Budget, paid media assumptions, content capacity en third-party costs.
- Label, distributor, publisher, PR, playlisting, sync, live of brand obligations.
- Proposed release date, pre-save timeline, pitch deadlines en campaign milestones.

## Workflow stappen

### 1. Bevestig release objective

Definieer wat de release moet bereiken: audience growth, fan conversion, brand positioning, cataloguswaarde, touring support, sync fit, partnership leverage of commercial revenue.

### 2. Check rights en metadata readiness

Controleer Master Rights, Publishing Rights, split sheets, samples, credits, metadata, distribution requirements en approvals voordat externe deadlines worden bevestigd.

### 3. Definieer positioning en messaging

Koppel release story aan artist positioning, Brandbook, audience, cultural context en campaign proof points.

### 4. Bouw campaign architecture

Leg release date, milestones, content pillars, Asset deadlines, PR moments, playlisting, social formats, partner moments, live moments en post-release follow-up vast.

### 5. Bevestig budget en owners

Leg costs, budget approvals, vendors, owners, deadlines, dependencies en required evidence vast in ClickUp.

### 6. Manage Assets en approvals

Controleer artwork, visuals, video, copy, press Assets en public announcements op version control, rights, quality en artist approval.

### 7. Voer launch readiness review uit

Controleer distribution, links, metadata, Assets, approvals, copy, schedule, stakeholder updates, risk log en contingency actions.

### 8. Monitor release execution

Volg deadlines, approvals, content posting, PR, platform updates, partner commitments, artist capacity en issues tijdens release window.

### 9. Evalueer post-release

Review performance, learnings, content results, audience signals, stakeholder feedback, missed opportunities en next actions voor de artist roadmap.

## Beslismomenten

- Is release date haalbaar op basis van rights, Assets en distribution deadlines?
- Is de release ready voor public announcement?
- Moet budget worden verhoogd, verlaagd of herverdeeld?
- Moeten release, content, PR of partnership moments worden gepauzeerd of verschoven?
- Welke post-release acties krijgen priority in de roadmap?

## Deliverables

- Release Strategie-document.
- Campaign timeline.
- Messaging en content pillars.
- Asset checklist.
- Rights en metadata readiness notes.
- Launch readiness checklist.
- Post-release evaluation.

## KPI's

- 100% van critical rights en metadata checks vóór delivery afgerond.
- Public announcement pas na documented approval.
- Alle campaign milestones hebben owner en due date.
- Launch readiness review afgerond vóór release week.
- Post-release evaluation binnen afgesproken termijn afgerond.

## Escalaties

Escaleren bij rights ambiguity, sample issue, missed distribution deadline, unapproved artwork, budget conflict, reputation risk, partner conflict, artist capacity issue, label/distributor blocker of public commitment zonder approval.

## Outputs

- Approved Release Strategie.
- Updated artist roadmap.
- ClickUp campaign tasks.
- Approved Assets en messaging.
- Post-release learnings en next actions.
