# 07 Deal Negotiation Flow

## Doel

Deze Workflow definieert hoe CM artist-gerelateerde deal negotiations bestuurt op Governance-niveau. Het doel is om opportunity fit, rights position, commercial terms, risks, approvals, negotiation strategy en documentation discipline te borgen.

De Workflow zorgt dat CM geen commerciële commitments doet zonder authority, legal review en duidelijke decision capture.

## Reikwijdte

Deze Workflow geldt voor label deals, distribution deals, publishing deals, sync opportunities, brand deals, live-related deals, catalogus opportunities, management-adjacent commercial terms en andere artist-business opportunities waar CM coördineert of onderhandelt.

CM levert geen juridisch advies tenzij expliciet bevoegd. Counsel owns legal advice, contract drafting en legal risk assessment. CM owns commercial coordination, process, briefing, negotiation tracking en artist decision support.

## Eigenaar

De artist manager of toegewezen deal lead owns de Workflow, negotiation tracker, communication log, decision log, term comparison, approvals en escalation. De artist owns final commercial approval. Counsel owns legal advice en contract language. Finance/accounting advisors own tax, accounting en payment treatment waar relevant.

## Inputs

- Opportunity brief, counterparty details en context.
- Artist goals, roadmap en constraints.
- Rights position: Master Rights, Publishing Rights, Neighbouring Rights en relevant ownership evidence.
- Existing agreements, exclusivity, restrictions, options, obligations en conflicts.
- Proposed terms, fees, advances, royalties, recoupment, term, territory, deliverables en approvals.
- Benchmark notes, red flags, counsel comments en negotiation history.
- Authority matrix en artist approval requirements.

## Workflow stappen

### 1. Registreer opportunity

Leg Lead, source, counterparty, deal type, status, deadline, owner, next action en confidentiality requirements vast.

### 2. Voer Fit Check uit

Beoordeel strategic fit, artist goals, brand fit, rights availability, timing, opportunity cost, reputation risk en commercial potential.

### 3. Verzamel deal inputs

Verzamel proposed terms, relevant agreements, rights evidence, deliverables, deadlines en open questions voor counterparty of counsel.

### 4. Identificeer red flags

Markeer exclusivity, rights transfer, long term, low compensation, approval limitations, broad usage, recoupment, audit limits, termination gaps en conflicts.

### 5. Bepaal negotiation position

Definieer target terms, walk-away points, must-haves, nice-to-haves, authority limits en escalation triggers.

### 6. Coördineer legal en specialist review

Brief counsel of specialist met context, documents, questions, deadlines en requested output. Vertaal advice naar artist decisions en negotiation points.

### 7. Onderhandel en documenteer

Leg proposals, counterproposals, calls, decisions, approvals en open issues vast. Bevestig materiële terms schriftelijk.

### 8. Bevestig final approval

Laat artist final terms, risks, obligations en signing authority schriftelijk approves vóór commitment of signature.

### 9. Archiveer en activeer obligations

Bewaar final documents in Google Drive, update ClickUp obligations, payment triggers, delivery deadlines, reporting needs en roadmap implications.

## Beslismomenten

- Past de opportunity bij artist goals en Scope?
- Is counsel review verplicht vóór verdere negotiation?
- Zijn rights beschikbaar en vrij van conflict?
- Welke terms zijn dealbreakers?
- Mag CM namens artist communiceren, onderhandelen of alleen coördineren?
- Is final approval volledig gedocumenteerd?

## Deliverables

- Deal opportunity record.
- Fit Check summary.
- Term comparison of deal memo.
- Red flags overview.
- Negotiation tracker.
- Approval record.
- Obligations tracker na closing.

## KPI's

- Geen deal commitment zonder documented artist authority.
- 100% van material terms is schriftelijk vastgelegd.
- Counsel review uitgevoerd bij legal risk, rights transfer of contract signature.
- Deal obligations staan binnen afgesproken termijn in ClickUp.
- Red flags zijn vóór final approval gedeeld met artist.

## Escalaties

Escaleren bij legal risk, rights conflict, exclusivity issue, unapproved commitment, counterparty pressure, payment risk, broad rights grant, reputation risk, missed deadline, unclear authority, tax/accounting issue of disagreement over walk-away point.

## Outputs

- Approved deal memo of negotiation close-out.
- Updated artist roadmap.
- ClickUp obligations en deadlines.
- Google Drive archive met final documents.
- Follow-up voor finance, legal, rights, release, marketing of partnership execution.
