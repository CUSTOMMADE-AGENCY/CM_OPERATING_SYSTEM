# Artist Client Workflow

## Purpose

Define the end-to-end artist client journey from first lead through renewal, upsell or archive.

## Workflow

1. Lead intake captures artist context, goals, urgency and budget range.
2. Fit check evaluates readiness, creative direction, business maturity and scope match.
3. Brand audit reviews positioning, visuals, narrative, channels and audience.
4. Brandbook phase converts positioning into practical creative and communication rules.
5. Business audit reviews rights, royalties, team, releases, contracts and operating risks.
6. Strategy scope defines roadmap, deliverables, timeline and decision owners.
7. Proposal translates scope into offer, fee, payment terms and assumptions.
8. Contract/payment confirms engagement before deep execution begins.
9. Onboarding creates Drive, ClickUp, meeting cadence and communication norms.
10. Execution delivers agreed workstreams through tracked tasks.
11. Delivery presents outputs and gathers approvals.
12. Review evaluates impact, risks and next priorities.
13. Close-out, upsell or archive completes the cycle.

## Storage and tooling boundaries
- GitHub stores this workflow, governance, SOP and system documentation.
- Google Drive stores live client folders, signed documents, deliverables and working files.
- ClickUp stores execution, pipeline stages, owners, due dates and task evidence.
- Gmail stores correspondence and approved communication templates.

## Repository hygiene
- Do not commit signed contracts, confidential client files, exports, duplicate folders or ad hoc drafts.
- Use clear version history through commits and pull requests instead of `final_final` filenames.
- Keep client-specific details in the appropriate live workspace, not in this repository.
