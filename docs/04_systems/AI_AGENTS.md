# AI Agents

AI agents may support drafting, summarizing, checklist generation, and operating-system maintenance. They must not be used as a storage location for confidential client files or signed agreements.

## Approved use cases

- Drafting workflow documentation.
- Summarizing non-confidential notes.
- Creating checklist variants from approved standards.
- Reviewing naming and structure consistency.

## Restrictions

- Do not upload signed contracts.
- Do not upload confidential client files unless a specific approved privacy workflow exists.
- Do not let AI outputs override approved legal, finance, or client decisions.

## Quality control

All AI-generated operating content must be reviewed by a human owner before it becomes an approved CM standard.
