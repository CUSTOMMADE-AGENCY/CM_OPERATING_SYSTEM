# Google Drive Structure

Google Drive is the source for live client folders, signed documents, deliverables, templates, assets, finance files, and legal files.

## Root structure

- `00_ADMIN` — company administration, internal references, and approved internal templates.
- `01_MASTER_BOUTIQUE` — Master Boutique transaction resources and non-confidential reusable templates.
- `02_ARTIST_MANAGEMENT` — artist-service resources, templates, brand assets, and delivery support files.
- `03_CLIENTS` — approved client folders and client deliverables.
- `04_DEALS` — live deal folders and transaction execution materials.
- `05_OPERATIONS` — operating support, meeting materials, and admin execution files.
- `06_FINANCE` — invoices, budgets, finance exports, and payment records.
- `07_LEGAL` — legal templates, signed agreements, and counsel-reviewed materials.
- `08_MARKETING` — public collateral, campaigns, and marketing assets.
- `09_CONTENT` — creative and content production materials.
- `99_ARCHIVE` — inactive clients, closed deals, old versions, and retired materials.

## Client folder template under `03_CLIENTS`

- `01_ADMIN` — onboarding, contracts, admin notes, and billing setup.
- `02_BRAND` — brand audit, brandbook, references, and positioning.
- `03_STRATEGY` — roadmap, strategy, and release planning.
- `04_DEALS_RIGHTS` — rights reviews, deal notes, and royalty support.
- `05_RELEASES` — release plans, timelines, and release assets.
- `06_CONTENT` — content planning, assets, and approvals.
- `07_FINANCE` — budgets, invoices, statements, and payment files.
- `08_DELIVERABLES` — approved final deliverables and handover files.
- `09_ARCHIVE` — inactive drafts and completed-phase material.

## Maintenance rules

- Use uppercase folder names.
- Avoid duplicate folders.
- Avoid deep unnecessary nesting.
- Store confidential and signed files in Drive, not GitHub.
